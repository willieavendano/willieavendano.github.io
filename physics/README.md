## Overview

A year-long, algebra-based physics course. Students learn to describe and predict the physical world with models, graphs, and equations — and to test those predictions in the lab. The course emphasizes reasoning from principles over formula-hunting: every unit is built around hands-on experiments, simulations, and real data.

## Course Outline

| Unit | Topic | Big Ideas |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Kinematics | Position, velocity, acceleration; motion graphs; free fall; projectile motion |
| 2 | Forces & Newton's Laws | Free-body diagrams; net force; friction; systems of objects |
| 3 | Circular Motion & Gravitation | Centripetal acceleration; universal gravitation; orbits |
| 4 | Energy | Work, kinetic and potential energy; conservation of energy; power |
| 5 | Momentum | Impulse; conservation of momentum; collisions |
| 6 | Rotational Motion | Torque; rotational equilibrium; angular momentum |
| 7 | Simple Harmonic Motion | Springs and pendulums; period and frequency; energy in oscillation |
| 8 | Waves & Sound | Wave properties; interference; standing waves; the Doppler effect |
| 9 | Electrostatics & DC Circuits | Charge; Coulomb's law; current, voltage, and resistance; series and parallel circuits |
