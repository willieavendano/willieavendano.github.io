Equation and meaning bank

For constant acceleration in one axis: vf = vi + at; Δx = vit + ½at²; vf² = vi² + 2aΔx; Δx = ½(vi + vf)t. Use Δy instead of Δx for vertical motion.

For launch angle θ measured above horizontal: vix = vi cos θ; viy = vi sin θ. Under the ideal projectile model, ax = 0 and ay = −9.8 m/s² with up positive. Axes share time, not velocity.

Fresh checks · solve before discussing

A. Stopping

A bicycle slows uniformly from 10.0 m/s to rest over 25.0 m. Find acceleration and stopping time.

B. Drop and launch

Two balls start 1.80 m above a level floor. One is dropped and the other launched horizontally at 3.0 m/s at the same instant. Find when each hits and the horizontal distance traveled by the launched ball. Explain the comparison.
