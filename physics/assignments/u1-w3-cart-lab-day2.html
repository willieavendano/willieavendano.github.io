3 · Slope → acceleration

Units the whole way · 10 min
  1. Pick two points on your line, far apart — on the line, not data points. Write both with units.
  2. Compute the slope, rise / run, with its unit.
  3. Compare x = ½at2 with y = mx: the slope is ½a, so a = 2 × slope. Find a.
  4. Rule the steepest and shallowest lines that still fit. Find a for each; ± is half the difference.
