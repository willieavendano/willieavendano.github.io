The question: what is the relationship between position and time for a cart accelerating from rest down a ramp? Today you build, predict, run, record, and plot. Tuesday you read the acceleration off this data.

1 · Build the ramp

Cardboard · 15 min
  • Straight. A ramp that sags changes slope partway down, and then the acceleration is not constant. Fold the cardboard into a channel or support it underneath so a meter stick lies flat along it.
  • Shallow. Raise the top end only a few centimetres. The cart should take at least 2 seconds to roll the full length; if it is faster, lower the ramp.
  • Fixed. Tape both ends down, add side rails so the cart rolls straight, and put a backstop at the bottom. Once you start timing, the ramp does not move.

3 · Set up

Decide once, then stay consistent · 5 min
  • Origin at the release line; down the ramp is positive. Positions in metres, by meter stick.
  • Front of the cart on the release line every time. Let go — do not push.
  • Five evenly spaced tape marks, measured from the release line: 0.20, 0.40, 0.60, 0.80, 1.00 m if your ramp is long enough. On a shorter ramp use a smaller step, such as 0.15, 0.30, 0.45, 0.60, 0.75 m.
  • Each run times release → one mark. Switch jobs after every mark.
