What the midterm looks like. 7 problems and about 70–75 minutes of work in a 90-minute session. It opens with a Unit 0 warm-up; after that, most problems use tools from more than one unit, and it ends with two capstones that pull the units together. Part (a) of each problem is the way in. The equation bank is printed on the cover, and your handwritten half-sheet cheat sheet (one side of half an 8.5″ × 11″ sheet) comes too. The exam is graded on the 4.0 scale: Unit 0, Unit 1, Unit 2, and Protocol & Communication (K / D / E / A, units, signs, FBDs) are averaged.

How this guide works. The 15 problems cover every skill on the midterm, and the tag on the right says which units each one uses. Work every problem from a blank page with full K / D / E / A, then check yourself against the final answers on the last page, and fix anything wrong in a different color. That correction layer is what gets checked on December 7. H boxes are required for Honors; they are optional stretch for everyone else.
