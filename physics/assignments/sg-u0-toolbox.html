12Fermi: the class-time censusEstimation

Estimate: how many seconds of class time does a Cushman student sit through in one whole school year? Do not look anything up — that's the point. This problem fuses both halves of Unit 0: Fermi's method and the conversions that carry units through the math.

  1. Break the question into three or four quantities you can reasonably guess, and write your guess for each — with units.
  2. Multiply through (units travel!) and report an order of magnitude.
  3. Which guess are you least confident in? If it were off by a factor of 2, would your order of magnitude survive?
