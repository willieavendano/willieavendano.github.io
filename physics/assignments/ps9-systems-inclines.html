How to work this set. Systems: use the system view (total mass, external forces only) to find a, then one object's individual FBD to find T. Inclines: tilt your axes with the ramp — parallel component mg sinθ, perpendicular mg cosθ, and N = mg cosθ, not mg. When in doubt, sanity-check your trig at θ = 0° and 90°.
