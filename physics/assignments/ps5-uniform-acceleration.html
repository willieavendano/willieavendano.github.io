1Pick the equation — don't solveSelection rule

For each scenario, name the missing variable (neither known nor wanted) and write the equation you'd use. No arithmetic.

  1. A jet starts from rest and accelerates at 3.5 m/s² down 900 m of runway. You want its takeoff speed.
  2. A train slows from 40 m/s to 10 m/s over 60 s. You want how far it traveled.
  3. A dropped phone falls for 0.80 s. You want how far it fell.
  4. A sprinter reaches 9.0 m/s in 3.0 s from rest. You want her acceleration.

7Grade this workCritique

The problem: "A car slows uniformly from 20 m/s to rest in 5.0 s. How far does it travel while stopping?" A student writes:

Δx = vit + ½at² = (0)(5.0) + ½(−4.0)(25) = −50 m

  1. The equation choice is fine and a = −4.0 m/s² is correct. Find the actual error. (Hint: read the subscripts out loud, Workshop-I style.)
  2. Fix it: run the full KDEA and report the real stopping distance. Sanity-check the sign of your answer.
