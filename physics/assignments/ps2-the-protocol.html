8Dimensional detectiveUnits as evidence

A speed v is measured in m/s and a time t in s. For each expression below, work out the units it produces (show the cancellation) and say whether it could possibly compute a distance.

  1. v × t
  2. v / t
  3. t / v

One of the three produces the units of a quantity we'll meet in Unit 1. If you can guess its name, write it down — no penalty for guessing.
