What this course is

Introduction to Engineering Design

The design course of the PLTW pathway. Students learn the engineering design process by living it — technical sketching, Fusion 360 modelling, 3D-printed prototypes, tearing a real product apart to see how it was made, then designing something that moves.

Say the one-line pitch out loud, then stop. The units slide does the detail work.

How you are assessed

What counts

  • Something ships every week. Sketch portfolios, modelled parts, analysis reports, prototypes. Portfolio-based grading, not a bubble sheet.
  • The engineering notebook. Dated, in ink, audited at midterm and again in June. Professionals document as they work.
  • The product thread. The object you take apart in Unit 2 is the same one you repair and improve later — analysis feeds improvement, exactly as in industry.
The most common parent question lives here. Be concrete about what a grade is made of.

Late work & second chances

The policy, plainly

  • One week to submit late work for partial credit.
  • Late formative work can earn up to a 2.0.
  • After one week the highest possible grade is 0 — Complete/No Credit, or NTI if never turned in.
  • Extenuating circumstances: see me BEFORE the due date and we will work it out.

Score a 2 or lower on a written exam? Fill out the Resubmit Form, come to extra help, and earn back half the missed points. The original has to have been submitted on time.

Emphasise the BEFORE. Students who come early get help; students who come after get the policy.
