# Introduction to Engineering Design

**Tag:** PLTW · Engineering &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Teacher:** Willie Avendano &nbsp;|&nbsp; [wavendano@cushmanschool.org](mailto:wavendano@cushmanschool.org)

## Overview

Students apply the engineering design process to model and prototype solutions using 3D modeling software (Autodesk Fusion 360). Course emphasizes documentation, iteration, and technical drawing. Part of the PLTW Engineering pathway.

## Course Outline

| Unit | Topic | Big Ideas / Deliverable | When |
|------|-------|--------------------------|------|
| 1 | Design Process | Engineering notebook habits; iterative design; concept map | Aug 24 – Sep 4 |
| 2 | Technical Sketching | Isometric and orthographic sketching; **sketching portfolio (Fri Sep 25)** | Sep 7 – Sep 25 |
| 3 | Measurement & Tolerancing | Calipers and precision; dimensioning; intro tolerances | Sep 28 – Oct 9 |
| 4 | CAD Foundations | Fusion 360 sketches, constraints, features; **parametric part models** | Oct 12 – Nov 6 |
| 5 | Assemblies | Joints and mates; exploded views; bill of materials | Nov 9 – 20 |
| 6 | Reverse Engineering | Product dissection — visual, functional, structural analysis; **RE report + presentation (Fri Jan 8)** | Nov 30 – Jan 8 |
| — | Midterm week — review, notebook audit, assessment | | Dec 14 – 18 |
| 7 | Redesign | Product-improvement of the dissected product; drawings; **redesign presentation (Fri Feb 5)** | Jan 11 – Feb 5 |
| 8 | Capstone Design Project | Open-ended brief → ideation → CAD + drawings → prototype → **presentations (Apr 30 – May 7)** → school-wide **exhibition (Wed May 26)** | Feb 8 – Jun 2 |

Every week closes on a Friday deliverable (or Wednesday when Friday is a noon dismissal). TRIP week (Mar 15–19) and midterm week carry no new content. Full week-by-week pacing is in the Year Calendar below.
