## Pilot

Analyze one short sequence. Record timestamps relative to the identified edition. Distinguish observable description from interpretation. Revise ambiguous categories, retain the revision history, and reapply the revised protocol to the pilot.

## Credibility and limits

Compare competing interpretations, record counterexamples, and use a consistent procedure. Explain how the researcher's perspective shapes interpretation. If counting features, define denominators and coding rules before interpreting totals.

Any interviews, surveys, or experiments require a revised protocol and the school's applicable review process before recruitment or data collection.
