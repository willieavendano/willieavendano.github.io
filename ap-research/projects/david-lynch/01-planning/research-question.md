# Develop a researchable question

These are exploratory directions, not claims that a gap exists:

- How does a defined sound device relate to a defined narrative effect in selected sequences?
- How does a recurring visual motif change across a bounded set of scenes?
- How do two selected works handle a specific transition between narrative perspectives?

For each candidate, specify the work(s), unit of analysis, terms, evidence, and method. Avoid treating “Lynchian” as a self-explanatory category. Define it through scholarship or replace it with observable features.

## Selection checkpoint

- Can the question be answered through the available evidence?
- Does it invite analysis rather than a plot summary or biography?
- What has scholarship already established? What remains uncertain?
- Is the scope small enough to examine closely?
- Could the method produce evidence against the initial interpretation?
