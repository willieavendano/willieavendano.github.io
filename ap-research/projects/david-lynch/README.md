## Folder map

- `01-planning`: scope, question, milestones, proposal.
- `02-literature`: bibliography and source notes; `sources-and-access.md` is the verified source map.
- `03-methods`: sampling, definitions, coding, and pilot revisions; `method-development-plan.md` and `computational-strand.md` are the advisor drafts behind the compendium.
- `04-analysis`: scene evidence and analytical memos.
- `05-writing`: paper outline and drafts.
- `06-advising`: meeting notes and decisions.

The prompts are starting points, not established research gaps or conclusions. The student should select the question and develop her own argument. Dates and submission requirements must be checked with the course teacher before setting deadlines.

This folder is inside a website repository. Keep student identifiers, private feedback, consent records, and restricted materials outside the repository. Do not commit film files or copyrighted readings.
