## Overview

The virtual section of Cushman's **AP Statistics** course. Same curriculum, same pacing, and the same materials as the [in-person section](/ap-statistics/) — three contact hours a week, delivered as one asynchronous Monday plus two live sessions on Wednesday and Friday.

This page is the **standing reference for the course**: what week we're in, what the focus is, and where the slides live. It works when Veracross doesn't, and it is the first place to look on an asynchronous Monday.

## How This Course Works

- **Monday is asynchronous.** No live session. Work independently through the week's materials — the deck linked below, assigned practice, and any recorded walkthroughs. The week's focus in the calendar tells you exactly what to cover.
- **Wednesday and Friday are live**, 8:30–9:30. New instruction, worked examples, and questions from Monday's independent work.
- **Unit tests are taken in the Friday live session** of the week they fall in. The in-person section tests on Thursdays; this section has no Thursday meeting, so every test moves to that week's live Friday. The dates are in the calendar below.
- **Same materials as the in-person section.** Every slide deck below is the same one used in person, so nothing is missed by being remote.
- **The AP Exam is the same exam**, in the same College Board window, taken on the same digital Bluebook platform.
