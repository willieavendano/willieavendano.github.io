Late work & second chances

The policy, plainly

  • One week to submit late work for partial credit.
  • Late formative work can earn up to a 2.0.
  • After one week the highest possible grade is 0 — Complete/No Credit, or NTI if never turned in.
  • Extenuating circumstances: see me BEFORE the due date and we will work it out.

Score a 2 or lower on a written exam? Fill out the Resubmit Form, come to extra help, and earn back half the missed points. The original has to have been submitted on time.

Emphasise the BEFORE. Students who come early get help; students who come after get the policy.

What I expect

Four things

  • Come ready. Laptop charged, charger in the bag, something to write with. Borrowing every day shows up in your responsibility score.
  • Participate. Ask, answer, argue, help a teammate. There is more than one way to be in the room.
  • Phones away. In the pouch, on silent. Laptops are for the work in front of you.
  • Own the absence. Email me. You get two class days to make up missed work before the late policy applies.
Read these as commitments, not threats. Tone here sets the year.

Executive function · how to actually keep up

Running your own week

  • Write it down somewhere that isn't Veracross. A planner or a notebook. The act of writing the deadline is what makes you remember it — not the app that already knows.
  • Notes are taken, not received. Photographing the board is not notetaking. Rewriting an idea in your own words is where it sticks.
  • Start the night it is assigned. Ten minutes on the day it is given beats an hour the night before, every single time.
  • Ask before it is due, not after. See me BEFORE the deadline if something has gone wrong. I can help then. After, I can only apply the policy.
This is the slide that saves the most students. Spend a full minute on the planner point — the school checklist explicitly asks for notetaking and executive function.
