SILLY WALKS — SINGLE-MOTOR INTERLEAVED-FOOT WALKER
Prototype v1 • millimetres • Bambu A1 / P2S 256 mm build volume

STATUS
Printable CAD prototype, not a fit-tested or load-tested robot. Motor confirmed:
V5 11W, green 18:1. Brain nominal dimensions are manufacturer specifications.
Motor and battery blocks in STEP/viewer are provisional layout envelopes.
Measure these before printing the whole chassis. No electronics included.

MECHANISM
Two cross-body feet alternate support. Each foot has two crank pivots 101.6 mm
apart. Four 12 mm-radius cranks give 24 mm lift range relative to chassis.
The two cranks on the same side must point in the SAME direction. The opposite
side is 180 degrees out of phase. A 1:1 chain drive between the two shafts keeps
them synchronized through dead centre. Do not omit the chain: the free linkage
can toggle or jam. These are feet; no rotating part contacts the floor.
The chassis rises/falls while stepping. Rubber grip strips are needed on bars.
Forward travel, speed, load capacity and traction require physical testing.

PRINT QUANTITIES
1 brain_deck; 1 side_wall_left; 1 side_wall_right; 1 motor_sled.
1 foot_left; 1 foot_right; 4 crank_x4; 4 pivot_spacer_x4.
1 fit_coupon. Motor shims: print thicknesses needed after measuring.
A filename ending x4 contains ONE part: print four copies.
Largest foot print footprint: 180 x 184 mm. Deck: 180 x 160 mm.
Assembled moving footprint is approximately 204 x 202 mm, excluding hardware.

FIRST PRINT
Print fit_coupon. Square holes, left to right along its long axis, are 3.30,
3.40 and 3.50 mm; last round hole is 5.00 mm. Test a standard 1/8-inch VEX steel
shaft (3.175 mm across flats). Use a snug sliding square fit, never a forced fit.
If needed, edit SHAFT_SQUARE in build_model.py and regenerate before printing arms.
5 mm round bores in the walls support rotating square shafts at their corners;
these are basic printed bushings and will wear. Ream carefully until free-running.

PRINT SETTINGS — STARTING POINT
PETG preferred for chassis and feet; PLA suitable for an initial fit prototype.
0.4 mm nozzle, 0.20 mm layers, 5 walls, 6 top/bottom layers, 35–40% gyroid.
Cranks: 6 walls and 100% infill. Deburr all holes without enlarging shaft flats.
Keep supplied STL orientations. Feet print bar-side down with tall rails upright;
use a brim. Side walls lie with their broad outside face on the bed. Enable
localized supports only if the slicer flags horizontal bores/overhangs. Inspect
the sliced layers before printing. All parts fit separately; do not print the
whole STEP assembly as one fused object.

NONPRINTED HARDWARE
- Existing V5 Brain, V5 11W motor, battery, power cable and Smart Cable (port 1).
- Two standard 1/8-inch square steel shafts, nominal 180 mm long; confirm final
  engagement before cutting. Ends must finish flush inside the crank outer faces
  (Y +/-91 mm) and MUST NOT project into the foot rails at Y +/-93 mm.
- Two equal VEX sprockets and matching chain, one on each shaft, at Y = -60 mm.
  Shaft centres are 101.6 mm apart. The STEP's 28 mm diameter discs are space
  reservations, NOT manufactured sprocket geometry. Use compatible sprockets
  whose outside radius is <=18 mm; measure chain and tensioner clearance.
- A compatible chain tensioner/idler and mounting adapter if required. The wall
  has a 3.4 x 22 mm adjustment slot; no chain tensioner is supplied in this model.
- Four shaft collars, fitted inside the side walls, clear of chain and motor.
- Four M3 x 25 mm pivot screws (smooth shoulder preferred), eight washers,
  four nyloc nuts. Four printed 2 mm spacers separate cranks from foot rails.
- Four M3 grub screws, approximately 6 mm long, to retain cranks on shafts.
  Cranks have 2.6 mm radial pilot holes: tap M3 gently; do not overtighten plastic.
- Four M3 x 20 mm deck screws and nuts; four M3 x 16 mm sled screws and nuts.
- Two hook-and-loop straps for Brain, two for motor, two for battery; approx.
  10–15 mm wide, length to suit. Thin foam liner and thin rubber foot grip tape.
Use nylock nuts on moving joints so screws can remain free-turning.

FIT CHECKS BEFORE FULL PRINT
1. Brain nominal 139.7 x 101.6 x 33.02 mm, screen upward. Cradle opening approx.
   144 x 108 mm, with only corner lips. Add about 2 mm foam below. Strap around
   the outer edges; keep display, power button, connector latches and cable bends
   unobstructed. This is an open protective cradle, not a sealed enclosure.
2. Motor nominal layout envelope is 76 x 65 x 48 mm, NOT measured supplier CAD.
   Actual motor must fit on the 86 x 140 mm sled. Its output shaft centre must
   align with X=-50.8, Z=54 mm. Sled top is Z=34 mm: nominal axis offset 20 mm.
   Measure actual offset, orient housing, then add shims only as needed. If your
   motor axis cannot reach 54 mm without interference, revise the sled geometry;
   do not bend the shaft or force the motor to align. Straps hold the housing.
3. Battery provisional space is 45 x 145 x 30 mm beneath the right half of deck.
   Measure actual battery and leads; adjust strap location and envelope first.
4. Sprocket, motor, collars and cables must all clear the feet throughout a full
   hand-turned revolution. Keep cables above the walking crossbars.

ASSEMBLY
1. Attach sled onto lower wall flanges. Fit motor/shims loosely. Install shafts
   through printed bushings, motor output and matching sprockets. Align motor
   before tightening straps. Add collars to prevent shafts sliding.
2. Align both shafts and fit chain. Maintain 1:1 phase; add compatible tensioner
   if needed. The chain must turn without binding and cannot touch the feet.
3. Fit the four cranks: matching direction on each side, 180 degrees opposite
   left/right. Their hub centres are at X +/-50.8, Z54. Fit grub screws.
4. Connect feet via M3 pivots, 2 mm spacers and washers. Three-bar foot goes left;
   two-bar foot goes right. Leave pivots free-moving and secure with nyloc nuts.
5. Hand-turn a full cycle before adding electronics. Check feet never collide,
   shafts do not protrude into rails, and chain cannot skip or rub the body.
6. Strap battery below deck; attach deck; add foam, Brain and edge straps.
   Connect motor to port 1. Fit rubber strips beneath all five contact bars.
7. Support the chassis for the first powered check, then test on a clear floor.
   Start with 10% velocity for 2 seconds. Stop if it binds, stalls or tips.
   Increase gently only after free movement. The earlier 25% / 5 second code
   was a motor test, not a validated loaded walking speed.

VALIDATION LIMITS
CAD validity, watertight meshes and bed dimensions are checked automatically.
The motion check samples 24 phases for printed feet vs feet/frame interference.
It does not certify metal hardware clearances, electronics fit, chain tension,
strength, torque, centre of mass, friction, battery retention or real walking.
Animation prescribes crank motion and is not a dynamics simulation.
