📐 Business Math Formula Sheet

Keep this tab open while you work. All formulas, definitions, and vocabulary you need!

💰 Revenue (also called Sales or Income)

Revenue = Price Per Cup × Cups Sold
Example: $1.50 × 40 cups = $60.00 Revenue

Revenue = total money that comes in from customers. It does NOT mean profit — costs still have to be subtracted!

📦 Variable Costs

Variable Costs = Cost Per Cup × Cups Sold
Example: $0.35 × 40 cups = $14.00 Variable Costs

These CHANGE based on how many cups you make/sell. More cups = more lemons, sugar, ice, cups. If you sell 0, variable cost = $0.

🔒 Fixed Costs

Fixed Costs = Same $ amount every day, regardless of cups sold
Example: Table rental $5/day — same whether you sell 0 cups or 200!

You pay fixed costs no matter what. This is why businesses must reach a certain sales level just to break even.

💸 Total Costs

Total Costs = Variable Costs + Fixed Costs
Example: $14.00 + $15.00 = $29.00 Total Costs

🏆 Profit (Net Income)

Profit = Revenue − Total Costs
Example: $60.00 − $29.00 = $31.00 Profit

If this is negative, it's called a loss. The goal of any business is to be profitable!

⚖️ Break-Even Analysis

Contribution Margin = Price Per Cup − Variable Cost Per Cup
Break-Even Cups/Day = Daily Fixed Costs ÷ Contribution Margin
Example: $15.00 ÷ ($1.50 − $0.35) = $15.00 ÷ $1.15 = ≈ 13 cups to break even

Every cup sold above break-even generates pure profit (after covering fixed costs). Below break-even = you're losing money that day.

📊 Profit Margin %

Profit Margin = (Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100
Example: ($31 ÷ $60) × 100 = 51.7% Profit Margin

Tells you: of every $1.00 in sales, how many cents become profit? A 51.7% margin means you keep $0.52 from every $1.00 earned.

📉 Variance Analysis

Variance ($) = Actual − Projected
Variance (%) = (Variance ÷ |Projected|) × 100
Example: Actual $280 − Projected $300 = −$20 (−6.7% variance)

Positive variance = did BETTER than expected. Negative = did WORSE. Real companies track this every month to improve their forecasting accuracy.

📦 Inventory Calculations

Units To Buy = max(0, Qty Needed − Qty On Hand)
Purchase Cost = Units To Buy × Cost Per Unit
Example: Need 20 lemon bags, have 5 → buy 15 × $2.99 = $44.85
