Financing a Car

Computer Science Math · 2026–27

Interest is the same machine whether it is paying you or charging you. Meet it as a friend first, then as a creditor.

Concept

Deck Interest Works For You First not built yet

Before borrowing, save. A high-yield savings account is compound interest pointed at you: the same formula, the same curve, the opposite sign. Understanding growth from the friendly side makes the amortisation table obvious instead of frightening.

Deck The Amortisation Machine not built yet

A loan payment is one number that hides two: interest owed on the remaining balance, and principal that actually reduces it. Watching the split shift month by month is the whole lesson.

Practice

HYSA → Loan Practice — csm-p2-interest-practice.xlsx

  1. HYSA: $500 at 4.5% APY, compounded monthly. Build the growth table for 5 years and chart it.
  2. Change compounding from monthly to daily. Explain in one sentence why the difference is small.
  3. Now flip the sign: the same machine as a $12,000 loan at 7% for 60 months. Build the payment formula.
  4. Split one payment into interest and principal. Copy down 60 rows and chart the crossover.
  5. Compare a 60-month and a 72-month loan on total interest paid. Which is the bigger number, and why is the smaller monthly payment the more expensive choice?

Build — what you hand in

Financing a Car (Thu Nov 12)

A model that takes MSRP, down payment, rate, and term as inputs and produces the monthly payment, a full amortisation schedule, and a defended recommendation of which financing option you would actually take.

Stretch (optional). Port the amortisation schedule to Python (a starting script ships with the kit) and confirm both produce the same payment to the cent.
