Personal Budget

Computer Science Math · 2026–27

You graduate, you get a job in Miami, and you pick a neighborhood. The rent is real, the salary is real, and the month does not care about your plan. Build the budget that survives it.

Concept

Deck The Hand You're Dealt not built yet

Fixed versus variable cost is the distinction the whole model turns on. Your randomly assigned role and neighborhood set the income and the rent — what you can change and what you cannot sets the strategy.

Deck Scenarios & the Savings Rate not built yet

A budget that only works in the expected month is not a budget. Scenario columns turn one plan into a stress test, and the savings rate is the single number that says whether you are actually getting ahead.

Practice

Miami Young-Professional Budget Practice — csm-p3-budget-practice.xlsx

  1. Roll your life: the randomiser assigns a job title, a starting salary, a Miami neighborhood, and a student-loan balance. Everyone gets a different hand, so nobody can copy.
  2. Look up your neighborhood's real median rent from the reference table and pull it in with a lookup — do not type it.
  3. Convert gross salary to take-home: federal tax, FICA, and the fact that Florida has no state income tax. Explain what that last one is worth to you in dollars per month.
  4. Classify twelve expenses as fixed or variable — then defend two you found genuinely ambiguous.
  5. Build the savings rate as a formula, not a typed number.
  6. Draw a scenario: the randomiser fires a Miami curveball (hurricane deductible, car repair on the 826, rent hike at renewal, hours cut, a bonus). Show the month under baseline and under the curveball, side by side.
  7. Find the break point — how many months of the bad scenario before savings hit zero?

Build — what you hand in

Personal Budget (Thu Dec 10)

A budget model built on your assigned Miami hand: take-home pay derived from gross, neighborhood rent pulled by lookup, categorised spending, a computed savings rate, at least three scenarios including one that breaks the budget, and a stated plan for the break.

Stretch (optional). Compare two neighborhoods on the same salary. Does the cheaper rent actually win once commute cost and time are priced in?
