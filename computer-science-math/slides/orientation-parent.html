How you are assessed

What counts

  • Cornerstone projects. Seven of them. Each is a working model plus a presentation. Rubrics are handed out the day the project starts, never after.
  • Formula honesty. A graded criterion. Typing a number where a formula belongs costs you, even when the number is right.
  • The Investopedia challenge. $100,000 of simulated capital across six market verticals. Graded on the reasoning you recorded before you bought — a well-argued loss beats a lucky win.
The most common parent question lives here. Be concrete about what a grade is made of.

Late work & second chances

The policy, plainly

  • One week to submit late work for partial credit.
  • Late formative work can earn up to a 2.0.
  • After one week the highest possible grade is 0 — Complete/No Credit, or NTI if never turned in.
  • Extenuating circumstances: see me BEFORE the due date and we will work it out.

Score a 2 or lower on a written exam? Fill out the Resubmit Form, come to extra help, and earn back half the missed points. The original has to have been submitted on time.

Emphasise the BEFORE. Students who come early get help; students who come after get the policy.
